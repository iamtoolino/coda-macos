Made for listening

Pick an album. Stay awhile.

Original-quality, gapless playback from your Navidrome or OpenSubsonic server.

  • Gapless, original quality. Your files play as they are, with no gaps between tracks.
  • Picks up where you left off. Continue Listening remembers your place in each album.
  • Colors from the cover. Every view takes on the artwork’s palette.
  • ReplayGain, if you want it. Album or track gain, chosen automatically.
  • Feels like a Mac app. Media keys, Now Playing, and Liquid Glass controls.
  • Private by design. No analytics or tracking. Coda only talks to your server.
